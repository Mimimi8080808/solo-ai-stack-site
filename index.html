The tool list

Video & podcast editingChecked Oct 30, 2026

Descript

Edit audio and video by editing the transcript, like a document.

Free
One-time 60 minutes of media, 100 AI credits, 720p export with a watermark.
Hobbyist
$16–$24 per person per month depending on billing. 10 media hours a month, 1080p without watermark, Studio Sound, Remove Filler Words, Create Clips.
For
Solo podcasters and YouTubers who record talking-head videos or interviews.
Skip if
Your videos have little speech, or you record under an hour a month (try Free first).

AI voiceChecked Oct 30, 2026

ElevenLabs

Turn written text into speech for videos, tutorials and product demos.

Free
10k credits a month, up to 3 Studio projects. No commercial license listed.
Starter
Adds a commercial license, Instant Voice Cloning, Dubbing Studio and 20 Studio projects.
For
Creators and course builders who need narration without recording every take.
Skip if
You post a few clips a month and are fine recording your own voice. For client work, budget for a paid plan.
ElevenLabs pricing page →
Meeting notesChecked Oct 30, 2026

Otter.ai

Record and transcribe calls, then ask an AI questions about the transcript.

Free
300 minutes a month, 30 minutes per conversation, 3 file imports in total.
Pro
$16.99 a month, or $8.33 a month billed yearly. 1,200 minutes a month, 90 minutes per conversation, 10 imports a month.
For
Freelancers and consultants with regular client calls.
Skip if
You rarely have calls, or mainly transcribe long uploaded files.
Otter.ai pricing page →
SchedulingChecked Oct 30, 2026

Calendly

A booking page so clients pick a time that fits your calendar.

Free
One event type, one calendar connection, booking page.
Standard
$10 per seat a month. Unlimited event types, six calendars, reminders, Stripe and PayPal payments, no Calendly branding.
For
Freelancers, coaches and consultants who book calls often.
Skip if
You book a few calls a month with one meeting type.
Calendly pricing page →
WritingChecked Oct 30, 2026

Grammarly

Checks spelling, grammar and tone as you type in email, docs and the browser.

Free
Spelling and grammar fixes, tone detection, 100 AI prompts a month.
Pro
$12 per member a month. 2,000 prompts, tone adjustment, full-sentence rewrites, plagiarism and AI-text detection.
For
Freelancers writing proposals and client emails, especially in a second language.
Skip if
You only need typo checks.
